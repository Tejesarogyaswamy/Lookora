✦ Platform features

Everything your customers need to say yes

From live AR to AI styling — the most complete virtual jewelry experience, built for indie designers.

Live face tracking AR

60fps real-time earring try-on. Tracks head turns, tilts, and zoom perfectly using MediaPipe.

GLB 3D file upload

Upload your 3D jewelry files under 6MB. Instantly AR-ready — no conversion or technical skills needed.

AI style recommender

Customer uploads outfit photo — AI instantly suggests the best jewelry from your catalog to match.

Custom HDRI lighting

Upload your own .hdr environment maps. Studio, golden hour, showroom — make jewelry shine your way.

Diamond & gem quality

Control cut grade, clarity, color, fire, and brilliance. Photorealistic gems that customers fall in love with.

Mix & match mode

Try earrings, necklace, and ring all at once in a single session. The full look — before buying.

Skin tone matcher

AI reads skin tone from camera and recommends whether yellow, rose, or white gold suits the customer best.

QR for physical stores

Print a QR code for your store counter. Customers scan it and try on jewelry live — offline meets online.
